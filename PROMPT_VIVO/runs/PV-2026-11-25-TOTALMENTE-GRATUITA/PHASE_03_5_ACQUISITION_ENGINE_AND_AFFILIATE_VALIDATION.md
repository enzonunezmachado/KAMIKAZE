# PHASE 03.5 — ACQUISITION ENGINE AND AFFILIATE VALIDATION

- Status: IN_PROGRESS / CONTINUE
- Created: 2026-11-26
- Purpose: reconstruct the mechanisms by which people discover, become interested in, trust and buy an offer; design a sustainable, low-cost multichannel acquisition operation.
- Immediate constraint remains: target at least R$150 by 2026-12-06, starting with R$0. Affiliate payout timing may make this route unsuitable for the immediate deadline.

## Core model: acquisition and conversion
1. Discovery: search, social feed, recommendations, communities, creators, direct referral or relevant outreach.
2. Attention: views/reach, watch time, profile visits and content retention.
3. Interest: follows, saves, meaningful comments, repeat visits, questions and requests for examples.
4. Intent: link clicks, product-page visits, direct inquiries, requests for details, checkout starts where observable.
5. Conversion: approved purchase, net commission, refund/chargeback and payout.
6. Retention/referral: repeat purchases, recommendations and additional products where applicable.

Views/followers are leading indicators, not purchases and not automatically vanity metrics. Always normalize by time, source, audience fit and downstream progression. A target like 23 qualified followers in one week is an experiment hypothesis, not a universal benchmark.

## Workstreams
A. Affiliate offer discovery:
- Hotmart, Kiwify, Eduzz and other legitimate platforms.
- Find products with a specific buyer problem, credible creator, transparent sales page, useful product preview, fair guarantee/refund terms and adequate customer support.
- Compare ticket price, gross and net commission, attribution model/cookie window, approval friction, payout schedule/minimum, refund/chargeback exposure and traffic restrictions.
- Prefer offers the operator can understand, evaluate and explain honestly; do not promote solely because commission is high.
- Investigate whether the buyer is already searching for the solution and what alternatives/competitors exist.

B. First-sale mechanism reconstruction:
- For each case, map product, buyer, trigger/problem, content/offer, channel, call to action, proof/trust, path to checkout, time-to-sale, price/commission, follow-up and payment evidence.
- Separate creator self-report, visible audience signals, platform analytics shared publicly, independent corroboration and verified payment.
- Do not discard self-reports; do not inflate them into verified facts.

C. Organic multichannel presence:
- Research native formats and audience behavior on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp/Telegram where relevant.
- Study profile setup, positioning, bio/link, pinned content, stories, cadence, repurposing and platform-specific creative adaptation.
- Avoid mass-copying identical posts; create native variants.

D. Community participation:
- Map groups by niche, audience, activity, buyer intent and explicit rules.
- Distinguish help/feedback channels, recommendation requests, buying-intent posts and promotional threads.
- Participate transparently and contribute first; disclose affiliate relationship where required; do not disguise as an ordinary buyer or seed fake conversations.
- No group is approved until its current rules are checked.

E. Direct distribution:
- Identify relevant potential buyers and public buying signals.
- Test the logic of individualized, contextual outreach; no bulk DMs, scraping or unsolicited spam.
- Respect platform rules, privacy, consent and opt-outs.

F. Operating system and facilitation:
- Build a daily/weekly workflow with a small number of repeatable actions.
- Prepare prompts, content briefs, post variants, story scripts, comment-response templates, research logs, link tracking and checklists.
- Human review before publishing; no autonomous posting or messaging.
- Prefer free tools and low-friction formats; protect NEXUS and avoid assuming spare AI Studio quota.

G. Measurement:
- Track per channel and content: reach/views, watch time if available, profile visits, follows, saves, meaningful comments, outbound clicks, conversations, qualified leads, purchases, refunds and net payout.
- Record date, content format, topic, CTA, audience segment and time spent.
- Evaluate conversion ratios only with denominators and time windows; avoid comparing raw views across different platforms as equivalent.

## Round 29 — relationship-led referrals and GitHub tooling (2026-11-26)

### Referral mechanism: relationship-led introduction
The user proposed approaching professionals who work with the relevant audience, presenting an existing affiliate product and the honest story behind the project, asking for feedback, and—if the offer is not relevant to them—asking whether they know someone who may benefit. The goal is to build a real opportunity through trust, not to pressure the first person into a sale.

Operational design:
1. Select professionals because they genuinely serve or understand the intended audience, not merely because they have many contacts.
2. Start with context and permission: explain why their opinion is relevant and ask whether they are open to a short overview.
3. Present the product accurately: what it does, who it helps, price, limitations, alternatives, refund/guarantee terms, and why it was selected.
4. Disclose clearly if the link is an affiliate link and that a commission may be earned.
5. Ask for feedback first. A professional may identify a better fit, a weakness, or a reason not to recommend it.
6. If it is not relevant to them, ask gently whether they know one person or role who might genuinely benefit. Make declining effortless.
7. Prefer a warm introduction: ask whether they would be comfortable forwarding a short message or introducing both parties. Do not request or store a third party's phone/email without their consent.
8. Provide a concise forwardable note containing the product, intended user, reason for relevance, affiliate disclosure and a no-pressure CTA.
9. Thank them regardless of outcome. No guilt, manufactured urgency, false friendship, implied debt, or repeated follow-ups after a no.
10. Record the source and outcome with minimal personal data: conversation, permission to introduce, intro sent, reply, qualified interest, click, sale, commission, and feedback.

The user's story about starting the project independently to help pay for college may be shared when relevant and truthful, but never used to create obligation or emotional pressure. This is a relationship and trust mechanism, not covert manipulation.

### Current design implication
The MAK should not be a single autonomous bot. Compose a light operating system from reusable components: research/offer verification, content planning and repurposing, human-reviewed publishing, relationship/intro tracking, link/click measurement and weekly learning. Automate preparation and organization first; keep outreach, introductions and publishing under human control.
