- Immediate objective: identify a realistic, fully digital, at-home path to earn the first R$150 by 2026-12-06, starting with R$0.

## Core questions
1. What can a beginner sell online from home without inventory, ad spend or paid tools?
2. Which routes can produce a first payment within days, and which are medium/long-term assets?
3. How do small creators with low view counts describe their first real sales? What is independently verifiable versus self-reported?
4. What is the real bottleneck: product, buyer access, trust, traffic, conversion, eligibility or payout delay?
5. Which legitimate remote tasks/studies accept Brazil and pay quickly enough to matter?
6. Which schemes are scams, pyramids, deceptive coaching or fake task platforms?

## Workstreams
A. Direct digital service sales: microdeliveries, fixed-scope packages, remote admin, research, document/ spreadsheet work, content operations, editing, QA and other sellable outcomes.
B. Digital products: templates, spreadsheets, guides, mini-products, pre-sales and marketplaces.
C. Affiliate marketing: product selection, approval, commission attribution, organic distribution, small creator case studies, conversion evidence and payout timing.
D. Audience/distribution: SEO/search intent, short-form video, YouTube niche channels, social selling, communities, referrals, partnerships and direct outreach where permitted.
E. Paid research and task platforms: Prolific, Respondent, UserTesting, uTest, OneForma, Clickworker, Toloka and legitimate AI data projects.
F. Scam and hype audit: pyramid economics, fake jobs, paid unlocks, coaching claims, screenshots, fabricated testimonials, undisclosed affiliate incentives and refund/payout traps.
G. Economics and fit: net R$, time-to-first-cash, skill requirements, probability/uncertainty, payout delays, age/country eligibility, equipment and zero-cost constraints.

## Initial findings (Round 25, S336–S360)
- Prolific lists Brazil as supported and requires 18+ and verification, but uses a waitlist; Brazilian users report highly variable study flow and modest early earnings. Not a dependable sole route to R$150 in ten days.
- Respondent is free to join and can pay for remote studies, but selection depends on profile; the platform says most participants receive 1–3 invitations monthly. Attractive study values are not guaranteed.
- UserTesting pays via PayPal, normally 14 days after a test, and requires fluency in English, French or German. Not a fit for the immediate goal or user's stated English level.
- uTest and OneForma pay on set cycles; OneForma has a US$10 minimum. Work is conditional on qualification and approval.
- Hotmart affiliation is free, but the platform supplies a tracking link, not an audience. Producer approval, commission rules, cookie attribution and refund/payout timing matter. No evidence yet that affiliate marketing is the fastest route without existing distribution.
- Small creators report first freelance/affiliate earnings, but the reviewed cases are self-reported and not independently verified. Useful lessons include a narrow niche, qualified audience, customized offer, portfolio proof and consistent content; not guaranteed formulas.
- A fixed-scope direct digital microservice remains the strongest immediate hypothesis because one direct buyer can cover the target, but no buyer has been identified or contacted.
- Exclude Hub Data from the fully digital route: it pays for video recordings of physical tasks and has device/task availability constraints.
- CVM warns against schemes funded by new participants' deposits, recruitment, high upfront fees and promises of easy/guaranteed returns.

## Round 27 — revised acquisition model (2026-11-26)
The user clarified that views are not purchases, but can be meaningful attention/interest signals; early follower growth is useful when normalized by time, audience quality and source. Do not dismiss self-reported first-sale cases solely because independent payment proof is absent. Reconstruct the claimed mechanism and label evidence strength, observable signals, alternative explanations and missing details.

The objective is now explicitly to understand and build an acquisition system, not merely choose a product or service:
- Discovery: platform/search/community and content distribution.
- Attention: views, reach, watch time and profile visits.
- Interest: follows, saves, meaningful comments, repeat visits and questions.
- Intent: product-page clicks, direct inquiries, link clicks, requests for details and checkout starts where observable.
- Conversion: completed/approved purchases, net commission/revenue and refunds.
- Retention/referral: repeat purchases, recommendations and follow-on sales where applicable.

Views and followers are leading indicators, not vanity by default; they become useful when tied to time, source, audience fit and downstream actions. A target such as 23 qualified followers in a week is a test hypothesis, not a universal benchmark.

Affiliate marketing is promoted from a generic medium-term idea to a priority investigation track because it avoids creating the initial product. It is not yet validated as the fastest cash route. Research must compare product-market fit, real buyer pain, quality and refund signals, commission net of platform fees where relevant, approval friction, attribution/cookie rules, payout schedule/minimum, allowed traffic sources, promotional assets and whether the offer can be explained honestly.

Design a sustainable multichannel operation: native content for multiple platforms, stories/short-form cadence, transparent and useful participation in relevant groups, individually relevant outreach only where permitted, and lightweight AI-assisted content preparation/checklists/copy templates. Do not use fake identities, fabricated testimonials, disguised promotion, spam, prohibited group promotion, scraping, or platform automation that violates terms. No account creation, affiliate application, publishing, contact or spend without explicit approval.

Official platform mechanics added as S380–S385: Hotmart offers free affiliate registration and product-specific links/commission/attribution rules; Kiwify offers a product marketplace with price/commission/category filters and product-specific application rules; Eduzz describes configured commission and last-click attribution. These sources establish platform mechanics, not product quality, demand, conversion or earnings.

Next: investigate product selection and actual affiliate case studies; reconstruct buyer journey from content to purchase; map permitted communities and channel-specific posting formats; compare organic distribution mechanisms and small-account growth trajectories; design the operating cadence and simple measurement sheet. Keep the immediate R$150-by-06/12 constraint visible, but do not assume affiliate payout will arrive by then.
